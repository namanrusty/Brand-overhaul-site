The Problem

How It Works

A controlled visit-triggered system that converts offline footfall into measurable repeat visits.

1. Scan

Customer scans a permanent QR placed inside the outlet.

2. Connect

Customer connects instantly on WhatsApp with structured opt-in.

3. Trigger

Visit-based logic activates a reward notification.

4. Confirm

Business validates redemption with minimal staff involvement.
